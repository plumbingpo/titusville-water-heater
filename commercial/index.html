Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main system. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, with service scheduled around your hours.

A dish room without enough hot water can't sanitize, and health inspectors check the handwashing sinks right away. We keep temperatures and recovery where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork shows a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Titusville Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Titusville Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Titusville and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Titusville Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
